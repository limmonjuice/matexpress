# MatExpress Storefront Home Page Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn `/` into a shop-first marketplace home page, modelled on the NovaTrend reference and adapted to construction materials from many sellers, delivered by MatExpress.

**Architecture:** A server-rendered page assembled from small section components in `src/components/home/`, fed by a static typed catalog. Only the interactive parts (search, menus, cart, saved items, countdown, product rows) are client components. The existing 3D truck story moves to `/how-it-works`, so the store loads no WebGL.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, plain CSS in `src/components/home/home.css` (`hm-` prefix, as `hero.css` and `story.css` do; Tailwind stays only as the reset), Zustand, GSAP and Lenis, Vitest with Testing Library. Runway MCP for sample imagery; `sharp` for WebP conversion.

**Spec:** the "Design brief" section below. There is no separate spec document.

## Design brief

### What the research says

| Finding | Source | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| 22% of sites hide search; users fall back on it when stuck | Baymard | Full search field in the header, not the reference's icon |
| 62% don't help users reach a well-defined scope from the home page | Baymard | Category tiles and a category menu high on the page |
| Feature at least 40% of product types so range isn't misjudged | Baymard | 8 of 12 categories as tiles (67%) |
| Static sections beat carousels; 75% of carousels were built wrong | Baymard | Static hero. Product rows scroll by hand, never by themselves |
| 43% of sites blur what's clickable | Baymard | One hit area per card; heart and add button clearly separate |
| Group menus at about 10 items, hover delay 300–500 ms, clickable headers | Baymard | Menu built to those numbers |
| "Best sellers" is an essential path to relevant products | Baymard | Keep the reference's Best Sellers section |
| Pop-ups read as spam; oversized promos draw negative reactions | Baymard | No overlays or newsletter pop-up; promos stay below the products |
| Bespoke imagery beats stock and bland cut-outs | Baymard | One consistent generated set, replaced by real photos before launch |
| 48% of industrial buyers order from a vehicle; speed and tap targets matter | BigCommerce | No WebGL on `/`, 44 px targets, WebP images |
| Buyers expect stock, tracking without calling, and quick reorder | BigCommerce | "Track order" in the header; reorder comes after accounts exist |
| Top bar carries location, tracking and delivery promise | Wilcon, Wickes, Zoro | Announcement bar, deliver-to selector, track-order link |
| Cards show the selling unit (`/ PC`, `/ BOX`) and stock | Wilcon | Price always carries its unit |
| Bright deals carry an honest end point ("Ends Wednesday") | Wickes | Countdown runs to a fixed date and never resets |

### Reference mapped to MatExpress

| Reference section | MatExpress version | Change and why |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement bar | Free delivery threshold, same-day dispatch cut-off, verified sellers | Same |
| Header with icons | Wordmark, deliver-to, **search field**, track order, saved, account, cart, then a category row | Search and location added |
| Hero with floating product cards | A site engineer on an orange arch, the approved headline, three floating product cards. Not the yard photo, per the user | Fashion model replaced by a site engineer |
| "Loved by 50,000+" | "Verified hardware stores near your site" | No invented customer count |
| Service strip | Compare suppliers · One checkout · Delivered to site · Live tracking | Marketplace promises |
| Shop by Categories | 8 tiles | 6 becomes 8 for range |
| New Arrivals | 6-card row: photo, badge, heart, seller, name, unit price, add button | Seller and offer count added |
| Best Sellers | 3 wide cards with Quick Add | Same |
| Flash Sale + New Collection | Dated deal with countdown + **Sell on MatExpress** | The second panel recruits sellers |
| Trust strip | Verified sellers · Secure payment · Proof of delivery · Support hours | Same |
| Footer (implied) | Shop, Sell, Help, payment methods | Added |

### Defaults that need your confirmation

1. **Confirmed by the user: the truck story moves to `/how-it-works`** (linked from the nav and hero), so products start right under the hero on `/`.
2. **Product cards get Quick Add**, as in this reference. This reverses your earlier "remove add to cart", which was about the old landing page.
3. **Product data stays static**, per your earlier instruction not to base product cards on the database.
4. **Motion follows the landing page, kept simple, per the user:** the hero enters with CSS keyframes, headings and card groups reveal once on scroll with GSAP, and Lenis smooths scrolling. Nothing is gated on reduced motion, per the user's earlier choice.

## Global Constraints

- Concept: a marketplace of hardware-company sellers; MatExpress handles delivery. Never mention Amazon. No trade account and no slab estimator.
- Palette, from the landing page: ink `#121925`, soft ink `#2a3240`, muted `#5b6472`, accent `#faa363` and `#f48b3e` with ink text, small orange text `#a84a0c`, page `#f7f4ef`, surface `#ffffff`, stone `#ece5dc`, tile `#f1eee8`, dark `#131925`.
- Plus Jakarta Sans only. Nothing readable under 13 px. Touch targets at least 44 px.
- Container `max-width: 1320px`, gutters 16 / 24 / 32 px at phone / tablet / desktop.
- `next/image` uses `preload` (not the deprecated `priority`) and default quality 75. Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using a Next API, per `AGENTS.md`.
- `/` imports nothing from `three`, `@react-three/*` or `src/components/journey/`.
- No auto-rotating content, pop-ups or overlays on load.
- All sellers, prices, ratings and dates are sample data, listed for replacement before launch.
- Runway spend stops at 200 of the 500 credits without asking the user.

5. **Search with spaces, no matches, or characters like `(` and `\`** doesn't crash, and offers a "Search all materials" route. Test in Task 4.
